Report an app issue

To help us understand the problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The MCIS MM app version.

  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Remove personal or confidential information from screenshots before sending them. Never send passwords or payment details.

Suggest a data correction

Found a place name, code, or administrative relationship that needs a closer look? Email us with the details:

  • The place name and associated place code shown in MCIS MM.
  • Its state or region, district, township, and other relevant parent divisions.
  • The proposed correction.
  • A link or reference to the source supporting the correction, if available.

Frequently asked questions

What does MCIS MM contain?

MCIS MM is a reference for Myanmar’s states and regions, districts, townships, towns, wards, village tracts, and villages, along with associated place codes.

What is a place code?

A place code is an identifier associated with a location. It helps distinguish places that may have similar or identical names. When reporting a record, include both its name and code.

What if a location or code appears incorrect?

Send the record and proposed correction using the instructions above. Administrative names, boundaries, and codes can change; verify information with the relevant issuing authority when accuracy is essential.

Can I ask a privacy-related question?

Yes. Email the support address above with “MCIS MM Privacy” in the subject. Please describe your question without including sensitive personal information.
