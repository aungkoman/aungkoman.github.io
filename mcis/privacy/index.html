Privacy Policy

MCIS MM does not collect user data and does not require sign-in.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes the privacy practices of the MCIS MM iOS app, developed by Aung Ko Man. MCIS MM provides reference information about Myanmar’s administrative divisions and associated place codes.

Data collection and accounts

MCIS MM does not collect personal information or other user data through the app. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use it.

Data use, sharing, and retention

Because the app does not collect user data, there is no app-collected user data for us to use, sell, share, or retain. We do not use app-collected user data for advertising or tracking.

Contacting support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this correspondence to respond to your question or support request. This is separate from using the app.

Please include only information needed to explain your request. You may contact us to ask for deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

External services and links

This policy covers MCIS MM. If you visit our website or use an external service, such as your email provider or the App Store, that service’s privacy policy applies to its own handling of information.

Changes to this policy

We will publish any changes on this page and update the effective date. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy to reflect those changes.

Privacy questions

Contact Aung Ko Man at admin@mmsoftware100.com with questions about this policy.
